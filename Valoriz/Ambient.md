
# Ambient – Retail POS and E-commerce Platform

**Organisation:** Valoriz Digital  
**Role:** Business Analyst  
**Project Area:** Retail Business Analysis, POS, E-commerce and System Integration

## Project Overview

Ambient involved POS (mobile and web) and e-commerce solutions designed to support retail operations. The work focused on defining business workflows, customer management, inventory processes and integration between retail systems.

## My Contributions

- Worked as the primary Business Analyst and point of contact for the POS workstream.
- Gathered and documented requirements for end-to-end POS workflows, including user login, start-of-day operations, billing and checkout.
- Defined business processes for promotions, coupons, cash payments, change and exchanges.
- Documented petty cash management workflows and daily financial tracking requirements.
- Defined customer management requirements covering customer onboarding, Customer 360 and account lifecycle management.
- Analysed GDPR-related requirements, including account deletion, communication preferences and audit trails.
- Defined customer account merging requirements across in-store POS and e-commerce systems.
- Documented inventory workflows covering purchase receipts, verification, stock counting and stock adjustments.
- Worked on POS and e-commerce integration requirements to support consistent customer and inventory information.
- Initiated analysis of e-commerce application features aligned with the wider retail POS ecosystem.
- Led client demonstrations and walkthroughs for POS and e-commerce workflows, gathering stakeholder feedback.

## Key Areas of Experience

- Business requirements gathering and documentation
- POS and retail operations
- E-commerce business processes
- Customer and inventory management
- GDPR-related business requirements
- System integration and workflow analysis
- Stakeholder engagement and client demonstrations
