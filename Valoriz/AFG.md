
# AFG – Enterprise Retail Application Support

**Organisation:** Valoriz Digital  
**Role:** Senior Support Engineer  
**Project Area:** Application Support, IT Service Delivery and Operational Improvement

## Project Overview

Provided L2 and L3 application support for retail systems used across multiple Al-Futtaim Group brands, including Marks & Spencer, Toys"R"Us, ACE and Watsons.

The role involved incident management, technical troubleshooting, operational coordination and collaboration with product and development teams.

## My Contributions

### Application Support and Incident Management

- Provided end-to-end L2 and L3 support for retail applications.
- Managed escalations, investigated production issues and worked towards meeting service level agreements (SLAs).
- Performed root cause analysis and coordinated fixes to reduce recurring incidents.
- Initiated client discussions to understand issues and identify their underlying causes.
- Supported database fixes, migration activities and e-commerce website troubleshooting.

### Operational Coordination and Leadership

- Coordinated offshore support activities and managed priority issues.
- Took responsibility as acting manager-in-charge during my manager's absence for approximately six months.
- Participated in sprint planning, change advisory board (CAB) meetings, retrospectives, backlog grooming and stakeholder workshops.
- Conducted knowledge-sharing sessions with team members.

### Automation and Process Improvement

- Used Python to automate recurring reports and operational tasks.
- Contributed to automation initiatives for product catalogue updates.
- Identified opportunities for UI/UX improvements based on incident trends.
- Supported reporting and operational process improvements.

### Business Analysis and Product Collaboration

- Proposed product enhancements and documented user stories for order management and POS systems.
- Conducted gap analysis and collaborated with product and development teams.
- Supported KPI definition, A/B testing and event-tracking activities.
- Contributed to product demonstrations, workshops and UAT planning.

## Key Areas of Experience

- L2/L3 application support
- Incident, problem and change management
- ITIL-based service delivery
- Root cause analysis

- Stakeholder and offshore team coordination
- User stories and product improvement
