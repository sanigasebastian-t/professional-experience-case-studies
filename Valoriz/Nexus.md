
# Nexus – SaaS Website Creation Platform

**Organisation:** Valoriz Digital  
**Role:** Business Analyst  
**Project Area:** SaaS Product Analysis, Requirements Engineering and UI/UX

## Project Overview

Nexus is a SaaS platform designed to enable users to create websites through a modular architecture. The platform was structured around four key components: Domain, Layout, Widget and Site.

## My Contributions

- Worked as the primary Business Analyst and point of contact for the project.
- Contributed to the Business Requirements Document (BRD), defining the platform's core structure and modular architecture.
- Created UI/UX designs for the websites and guided the development team during implementation.
- Participated in product and architecture discussions to help shape the platform.
- Defined requirements for key capabilities, including a centralised content management system (CMS), AI-assisted content generation and subscription models.
- Contributed to discussions around a multi-tenant structure supporting partners, organisations and end users.
- Participated in stakeholder demonstrations and product presentations.

## Key Areas of Experience

- Business requirements and product documentation
- SaaS product analysis
- Modular and multi-tenant platform concepts
- UI/UX design and development coordination
- Stakeholder communication and product demonstrations
